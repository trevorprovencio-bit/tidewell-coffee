Orders

Prices are in US dollars. An order is confirmed once payment goes through and you receive a confirmation email.

Shipping and pickup

Coffee is roasted in small batches, so some orders ship a few days after they are placed. Pickup orders can be collected from the location you choose at checkout.

Returns

Coffee is a fresh product, so we cannot take it back once opened. If something arrives damaged or wrong, tell us and we will put it right.

Catering

A catering date is held once the deposit invoice is paid. The deposit, balance and cancellation terms for your event are set out in your quote.

Changes

We may update these terms as the shop grows. The version on this page is the one that applies.

Privacy policy

What Tidewell Coffee Co. collects when you use this site, and what we do with it.

Draft, not yet reviewed

What we collect

When you place an order we collect your name, email, shipping address and order details. When you ask for a catering quote we collect the event details you enter. When you join the mailing list we collect your email address.

Payments

Card payments are handled by Square. Your card number goes straight to Square and is never stored on this site.

Catering requests

Catering quote requests are collected through Flashquotes, which stores what you submit so we can prepare and send your quote.

How we use it

We use your details to fill orders, answer catering requests and send the emails you asked for. We do not sell your information.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from the mailing list using the link in any email. To see or delete the details we hold about you, message us on Instagram at tidewellcoffeeco.

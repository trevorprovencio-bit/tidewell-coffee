Good to know

How far ahead should I book?

Two to three weeks ahead is a safe bet, and earlier for weekends in wedding season. Send the form even if the date is close and we will tell you straight away whether we can do it.

Where do you cater?

Across San Diego County. Put the venue or neighbourhood in the form and any travel cost will be shown in your quote.

What do you need at the venue?

A level spot about 6 by 6 feet, a standard power outlet within reach and a path we can wheel the bar along. Someone to point us to the spot on the day helps too.

Can you set up outdoors?

Yes. Beaches, backyards and car parks are all fine as long as the ground is level. If there is no outlet nearby, tell us in the form and we can bring a generator.

What kinds of events do you cater?

Weddings, office mornings, private parties, markets and pop ups. If people are gathering and want good coffee, we can probably do it.

Can you match the menu to our event?

Yes. We can name drinks for the day, add a seasonal syrup or trim the menu to keep the line moving. Put what you have in mind in the notes.

Can we have our own branding on the cups?

Yes, with enough notice. Ask for branded cups or sleeves in the form and we will price them into your quote.

How do I pay?

Once you accept the quote we email an invoice for the deposit, then another for the balance before the event. Both can be paid by card online.

Can you do non dairy and decaf?

Yes. Oat and almond milk come as standard, and our Mexico Chiapas decaf can be on the bar if you ask for it in the form.
